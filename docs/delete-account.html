Delete your Foodvexa account

You can request deletion of your Foodvexa account and the personal information associated with it. Requests are reviewed and processed manually while Foodvexa uses the free Firebase plan; opening WhatsApp or submitting a message does not delete the account immediately.

Option 1: Delete from the app

  1. Open Foodvexa and sign in with the Google account you use in the app.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm the request.
  4. If the app reports that deletion could not be completed, use the support option below.

Option 2: Request deletion through support

Contact Foodvexa support and write: “Please permanently delete my Foodvexa account and associated personal data.” Include the email address used to sign in so support can verify the account. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

Request deletion on WhatsApp Call support

What is deleted?

After the request is verified and processed, the sign-in account is deleted and personal contact and delivery details associated with the account are removed from saved order records where applicable.

What may be retained?

Order items, totals, and limited transaction records may be retained when needed for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or other legal obligations. Where records must be retained, direct personal contact and delivery details are removed where applicable.

Questions

Use the support options above if you need help or if in-app deletion does not complete.
